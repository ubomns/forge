## Sections
1. Problem — the user pain; evidenced, or labeled as an assumption.
2. Users / personas — who, and their context.
3. Goals & success metrics — measurable; what observably changes if shipped.
4. Scope — IN scope (bulleted) and explicit Non-goals (OUT).
5. Requirements — prioritized via MoSCoW (Must / Should / Could / Won't).
6. UX notes — high level only; defer detail to the designer.
7. Dependencies & risks.
8. Open questions.
9. Assumptions — each labeled "ASSUMPTION — verify".

## Quality bar
- Every requirement is testable.
- WHAT and WHY only, never HOW (no tech choices).
- No unlabeled guesses; metrics are real or explicitly marked assumed.
