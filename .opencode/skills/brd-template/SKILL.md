## Sections
1. Overview & objective.
2. Stakeholders.
3. Functional requirements — numbered FR-1, FR-2... for traceability.
4. Non-functional requirements — performance, security, accessibility,
   compliance; numbered NFR-1, NFR-2...
5. User stories — As a <role>, I want <goal>, so that <benefit>.
6. Acceptance criteria — Given/When/Then per story (see acceptance-criteria).
7. Data & validation rules — fields, types, constraints.
8. Edge cases & error handling.
9. Dependencies & assumptions.
10. Clarifications needed — open ambiguities (never invented answers).

## Quality bar
- Each requirement uniquely numbered and testable.
- No vague adjectives; quantify wherever possible.
- Ambiguities are listed, not resolved by guessing.
